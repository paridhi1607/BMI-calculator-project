#PROJECT STATEMENT
#PROJECT TITTLE 
**BMI CALCULATOR WITH BMR AND KCAL-COUNTER USING PYTHON**

#PROBLEM STATEMENT 
People this days are facing lot of health issues which can be controled by maintaining a healthy weight.This project solves the problem by providing- 
-instant BMI calculation
-clear health categorization 
-accurate BMR based on age, gender, lifestyle.
-daily calorie estimation using activity mulyiplier.

#SCOPE
The project accepts details such as age, gender, height, weight and gives detail about your health such as BMI, BMR AND Kcal to consume per day. 

#TARGET USERS 
1.Teenagers 
2.Young adults
3.Seniour citizens

#HIGH LEVEL FEATURES 
1.Health and fitness tracking
2.personal weight management 
3.understanding calorie requirements 
4.Educational use for beginners learning python.
